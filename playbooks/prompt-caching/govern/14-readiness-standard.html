14 · 15-Point Migration Readiness Standard (C1–C15) ​

1 · Start2 · Qualify & Size3 · Design4 · Validate5 · Govern
3 min readVerified 2026-11-19 PST
Why It Matters

Model cutover reviews stall without a single objective go/no-go checklist tying architecture, testing, and capacity sizing together.

Key Takeaway

Adopt C1–C15 as your standing release gate—mapping every architecture, CI lint, load-test, and PTU sizing check to concrete evidence.

Practical Application

Use this page (or the printable PR template) during Week 3 cutover verification before promoting to production.

Open the copy-ready PR / release-gate template subpage: C1–C15 Migration Readiness PR Checklist →

Adopt criteria C1–C15 as a standing release gate across the 7-layer pipeline before cutting over any production agent workload to GPT-5.6, GPT-6, or GPT-6.1 [Agree]:

For regulated audit, tax, legal, and compliance workpapers, augment C12 and C15 with the four Workpaper Defensibility Controls (C-WP1–C-WP4) in Regulated Multi-Engagement & Compliance Platforms.

Four-Stage Standing Governance Lifecycle [Arch] ​

  1. Stage I — Design & Lint: Map every prompt family to Pos 1–6 and enforce PCR001–006 in PR CI.
  2. Stage II — Shadow Eval: Execute the 2-pass DeepEval quality + cache contract suite on candidate models.
  3. Stage III — Capacity Calibration: Right-size base PTUs from 30-day sustained usage, empirical PeakNormTPM, and measured hit rates, paired with APIM Standard spillover.
  4. Stage IV — Continuous Assurance: Monitor Layer 7 OTel/KQL drift alerts across model snapshot updates.
